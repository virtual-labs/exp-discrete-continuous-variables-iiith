Deriving PMF/PDF from CDF

Instructions
    Procedure:
    • Click to generate a random CDF plot (either continuous Uniform or a discrete RV).
    • Identify the type of RV and select it from the dropdown.
    • For continuous RV: Enter the constant PDF value (the derivative of the slope) and the range [left, right] where it is non-zero.
    • For discrete RV: Enter the PMF values (the heights of the jumps) for each corresponding x-value.
    • Click "Submit" to check your answer.
