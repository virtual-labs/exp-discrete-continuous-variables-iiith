Bernoulli Random Variable

Instructions
    Procedure:
    • A Head is considered a "success" and is mapped to \(X=1\).
    • A Tail is considered a "failure" and is mapped to \(X=0\).
    • Choose a value for the probability of getting a Head, denoted by \(p\).
    • Enter the value of \(p\) in the input box and click "Set".
    • Click "TOSS" to simulate the coin flip and watch the animation.
    • Observe the outcome and read the detailed explanation in the "Observations" panel.
    • Click "Reset" to start over with a new probability.
